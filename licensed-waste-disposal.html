The Importance of Licensed Disposal: Protecting You and the Environment

Why checking for a valid Waste Carrier Licence like CBDU491542 is the most important step when booking a clearance.

Your Legal Responsibility (Duty of Care)

Did you know that as a householder, you are legally responsible for where your waste ends up? If you hire an unlicensed collector and they fly-tip your items, you could be the one facing a heavy fine or prosecution.

Why CBDU491542 Matters

Being a Licensed Waste Carrier means we are registered with the Environment Agency. This ensures:

  • Full Traceability: We provide Waste Transfer Notes showing exactly where your rubbish goes.
  • Responsible Sorting: We aim to recycle as much as possible, diverting waste from landfills.
  • Professional Standards: We operate safely and ethically within UK environmental laws.

Protecting the Environment

Fly-tipping isn't just a legal risk; it's a disaster for local wildlife and communities. By choosing Donnelly’s Recycling, you are ensuring that your old furniture, carpets, and appliances are handled by professionals who care about Nottinghamshire's environment.

Before you book anyone for a clearance, always ask for their licence number. At Donnelly’s Recycling, our licence (CBDU491542) is your guarantee of a job done right.

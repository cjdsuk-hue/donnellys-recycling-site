Frequently Asked Questions

What areas do you cover for house clearance and waste removal and rubbish removal?

We cover Nottingham and Nottinghamshire plus surrounding areas including Mansfield, Newark, Derby, Loughborough, Sheffield and Chesterfield. We also travel to nearby towns across the region—if you’re unsure, just contact us and we’ll confirm.

Are you a licensed waste carrier?

Yes, Donnellys Recycling is a fully licensed waste carrier (CBDU491542), ensuring all waste is handled and disposed of legally and responsibly.

How much does house clearance cost?

Costs vary based on the size and type of clearance. We offer free, no-obligation quotes—call us or send a message for a quick estimate.

Do you collect scrap metal for free?

Yes, we provide completely free scrap metal collection including appliances like washing machines, cookers, radiators, and more.

What kind of items can you remove?

We remove general household waste, furniture, garden rubbish, building materials, and more. If it’s non-hazardous, we likely take it.

What happens to the waste you collect?

We recycle or responsibly dispose of over 90% of all waste collected, helping reduce landfill and protect the environment.

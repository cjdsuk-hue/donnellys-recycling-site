Frequently Asked Questions

What areas do you cover for house clearance and waste removal?

We cover Nottingham and nearby areas, with dedicated pages for Nottinghamshire, Mansfield, Newark, Derby, Loughborough, Sheffield and Chesterfield.

Are you a licensed waste carrier?

Yes, Donnelly’s Recycling is a fully licensed waste carrier (CBDU491542), ensuring all waste is handled and disposed of legally and responsibly.

How much does house clearance cost?

Costs vary based on the size and type of clearance. We offer free, no-obligation quotes—call us or send a message for a quick estimate.

Do you take scrap metal?

Yes, any scrap metal can be taken away as a secondary service alongside our main house clearances and paid waste removals. For separate, larger volumes of scrap, view our dedicated scrap collection page.

What kind of items can you remove?

We remove general household waste, furniture, garden rubbish, building materials, and more. If it’s non-hazardous, we likely take it.

What happens to the waste you collect?

We recycle or responsibly dispose of over 90% of all waste collected, helping reduce landfill and protect the environment.

Commercial & Larger Project Soft Strip-Out Contractors

Managing a refurbishment, HMO turnaround or larger property project? Donnelly’s Recycling provides commercial and multi-room soft strip-out services, combining agreed internal removal work with licensed waste clearance.

We work with HMO operators, landlords, property managers, developers and refurbishment contractors who need a reliable clearance and strip-out package, with a clear scope and fixed-price quotation wherever the work can be assessed in advance.

Commercial Soft Strip-Out & Property Preparation

We provide combined clearance and soft strip-out services for larger residential and commercial property projects. Work is agreed in advance so the client has a clear understanding of what is included, how waste will be removed and what the quotation covers.

Our work is particularly suited to multi-bedroom properties, HMO refurbishments and projects where furniture clearance and internal strip-out need to be coordinated as one package.

HMO & Multi-Property Strip-Out Packages

We can quote for individual properties or discuss repeat work across a portfolio, subject to the location, access, programme and scope.

  • Bedroom furniture, contents and redundant fixtures
  • Carpets, underlay and gripper rods
  • Laminate, wooden flooring and floor tiles
  • Blinds, selected fittings and agreed internal finishes
  • Kitchen cabinets, worktops, splashbacks and floor tiles
  • Bathroom fittings, tiles and bedroom washbasins
  • Loading, transportation and disposal of the agreed waste

Kitchen, Bathroom & Specialist Disconnections

For suitable projects, the package can be extended beyond the initial clearance and soft strip-out to include complete kitchen and bathroom removal, together with agreed bedroom fixtures.

  • Kitchen cupboards, cabinets and worktops
  • Extractor fans and selected kitchen fittings
  • Bathroom suites, vanity units and tiles
  • Bedroom washbasins and associated fittings
  • Appliance removal following safe disconnection

Gas, electrical and plumbing disconnections are arranged through appropriately qualified contractors where required. These specialist elements are confirmed as part of the agreed project scope.

Flooring & Internal Strip-Out Work

We remove agreed non-structural internal finishes to prepare properties for refurbishment.

  • Carpets, underlay and gripper rods
  • Wooden and laminate flooring
  • Floor tiles and associated coverings
  • Blinds and selected window fittings
  • Skirting boards and agreed internal fixtures
  • Furniture and redundant room contents

Experience With Larger Multi-Bedroom Projects

Our project experience includes a six-bedroom property in Loughborough and a ten-bedroom property in Manchester, where clearance and agreed internal strip-out work were carried out as part of the refurbishment preparation.

For larger jobs, we look at the property size, number of rooms, flooring types and layers, volume of furniture, waste streams, access and programme before providing a quotation. This helps avoid treating every property as if it has the same workload.

Fixed-Price Quotes for Commercial & Larger Projects

We understand that larger projects need clear costs and an agreed specification. Wherever possible, we assess the work using photographs, videos, floor plans, room schedules and property details before arranging a site visit.

Our quotations can cover the agreed clearance and strip-out work, loading, transportation and waste disposal as one fixed-price package.

Quotes are based on the visible condition and agreed scope. Hidden or additional flooring layers, concealed materials, unexpected work, restricted access or changes to the specification may require a revised price.

Any specialist gas, electrical or plumbing work is confirmed separately and arranged through appropriately qualified contractors where required.

Preparing a property for refurbishment, renovation or a new tenancy? Donnelly’s Recycling provides professional soft strip-out services in Loughborough, combining internal removal work with licensed waste clearance.

Soft Strip-Out Services in Loughborough

A soft strip-out involves removing agreed non-structural internal elements before refurbishment, renovation or redevelopment begins. We can undertake individual rooms or combine several elements into one fixed-price property preparation package.

We have completed multi-bedroom clearance and strip-out work in Loughborough, including a six-bedroom property where the agreed work was completed in a day. Every quotation is based on the actual property, access and scope of work.

Flooring & Internal Finishes Removal

We remove existing flooring and selected internal finishes to prepare properties for the next stage of refurbishment.

  • Carpets, underlay and gripper rods
  • Laminate and wooden flooring
  • Floor tiles and associated coverings
  • Blinds and selected window fittings
  • Skirting boards and agreed internal fixtures
  • Redundant furniture and room contents

Kitchen Strip-Outs

Kitchen removals can be included within a wider Loughborough property strip-out or quoted as an agreed standalone element.

  • Kitchen cupboards and fitted cabinets
  • Worktops and breakfast bars
  • Splashbacks and wall tiles
  • Kitchen floor tiles
  • Extractor fans and selected fittings
  • Appliance removal, subject to safe disconnection

Bathroom & Bedroom Strip-Outs

We can remove agreed bathroom fittings and bedroom fixtures as part of a refurbishment package.

  • Baths, toilets and washbasins
  • Bathroom cabinets and vanity units
  • Bathroom wall and floor tiles
  • Bedroom washbasins and associated fittings
  • Selected bathroom fixtures and fittings

Gas, electrical and plumbing disconnections are arranged through appropriately qualified contractors where required.

Professional Soft Strip-Out Contractors in Nottingham & the East Midlands

Preparing a property for refurbishment, renovation or redevelopment? Donnelly’s Recycling provides professional soft strip-out services in Nottingham and across the East Midlands, combining internal removal work with licensed waste clearance.

From individual rooms and kitchen removals to complete HMO property preparation, we offer practical, fixed-price solutions for landlords, property managers, developers and refurbishment contractors.

Property Soft Strip-Out Services

A soft strip-out involves removing selected non-structural internal elements from a building before refurbishment or renovation work begins.

We can undertake individual removal requirements or combine several services into one larger property preparation contract. The exact scope is agreed before work starts.

Flooring & Internal Finishes Removal

We remove existing flooring and internal finishes to help prepare properties for new installations and refurbishment.

  • Carpets, underlay and gripper rods
  • Laminate and wooden flooring
  • Floor tiles and associated coverings
  • Blinds and selected window fittings
  • Skirting boards and agreed internal fixtures
  • Redundant furniture and room contents

Kitchen Strip-Outs

We provide kitchen removal as part of property refurbishment and soft strip-out projects.

  • Kitchen cupboards and fitted cabinets
  • Worktops and breakfast bars
  • Splashbacks and wall tiles
  • Kitchen floor tiles
  • Extractor fans and selected fittings
  • Appliance removal, subject to safe disconnection

Bathroom & Bedroom Strip-Outs

We can remove existing bathroom fittings and bedroom fixtures as part of a wider refurbishment project.

  • Baths, toilets and washbasins
  • Bathroom cabinets and vanity units
  • Bathroom wall and floor tiles
  • Bedroom washbasins and associated fittings
  • Selected bathroom fixtures and fittings

Gas, electrical and plumbing disconnections are arranged through appropriately qualified contractors where required.

HMO & Multi-Room Property Strip-Outs

We work on multi-bedroom properties and HMO refurbishment projects where several rooms need to be cleared and prepared for the next stage of renovation.

  • Bedroom furniture and contents removal
  • Carpets, underlay and flooring throughout
  • Kitchen and communal area clearances
  • Bathroom and bedroom fixture removal
  • Blinds, fittings and agreed internal finishes
  • Loading and removal of resulting waste

Commercial & Contractor Soft Strip-Out Services

In addition to residential properties, we undertake larger soft strip-out and clearance projects for commercial clients.

We can work with:

  • HMO operators and property management companies
  • Landlords and letting agents
  • Property developers and investors

  • Commercial property owners and tenants
  • Student accommodation providers

Whether you require several rooms stripped back or a larger multi-bedroom property prepared for refurbishment, we can discuss the scope and provide a tailored quotation.

Fixed-Price Soft Strip-Out Quotations

We understand that every property and refurbishment project is different.

Wherever possible, we prefer to assess the work using photographs, videos, property details or floor plans. This allows us to establish the requirements and provide a clear quotation without unnecessary site visits.

For larger projects, we can discuss a complete fixed-price package covering the agreed strip-out work, loading, transportation and waste disposal.

Our quotations are based on the visible condition and agreed scope of work. Additional layers, concealed materials, unexpected work or changes to the specification may require a revised price.

Specialist services, including electrical, gas and plumbing disconnections, are arranged separately through appropriately qualified contractors where required.
